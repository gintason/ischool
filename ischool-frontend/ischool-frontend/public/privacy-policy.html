Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · Applies to the iSchool website (www.ischool.ng) and the iSchool Mobile app (package ng.ischool.mobile), including iSchool Ola and iSchool Ole.

This policy is published by iSchool (“iSchool”, “we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable laws.

1. Who our services are for

iSchool Ola provides computer-based tests (CBT), revision and results for secondary school students (JSS1–SSS3). iSchool Ole matches students with teachers for live online lessons. Teachers can also apply to teach on iSchool Ole.

Many of our students are under 18. Accounts for students are created by, or with the permission of, a parent, guardian or school, who provides the registration and payment details. See section 9.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: full name, email address, phone number, username and password (stored only in encrypted/hashed form).
  • Verification: the phone number or email address you enter to receive a one-time sign-in code.
  • Learning profile: class level, chosen subjects, and for registrations made by a school or family, the school/parent name, state, location and contact email.
  • Test activity: your answers (multiple-choice selections and written theory answers), scores, results and the time taken.
  • Live lessons (Ole): class schedule, attendance records and lesson history.
  • Teacher applications: name, email, phone number, subjects, availability and the CV you upload.
  • Payments: subscription plan, amount, payment reference and status. Card and bank details are entered on Paystack’s secure page and are not received or stored by us.
  • Messages: anything you send us through contact forms or email.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data needed to run the service: app version, device and operating system type, and server logs (such as IP address, time of request and errors) used for security and troubleshooting.
  • The app stores your sign-in session on your device so you stay logged in.

We do not use advertising, advertising IDs, or third-party analytics or tracking tools in the app, and we do not collect your precise location, contacts or photos.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, verify your phone number or email, and sign you in.
  • To deliver tests, mark answers, calculate scores and email result reports to the student and, where provided, the parent/guardian or school.
  • To match Ole students with teachers, schedule live classes and record attendance.
  • To review teacher applications and manage hired teachers.
  • To process subscriptions and payments and confirm their status.
  • To send service messages (sign-in codes, login details, receipts, results and important notices).
  • To keep the service safe, prevent fraud and abuse, fix problems and improve our features.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We use your data because it is needed to provide the service you or your parent/school signed up for, to meet legal obligations, or for our legitimate interest in running a safe and reliable service. We do not sell personal data.

5. International transfers

Some of our providers process data outside Nigeria. Where this happens we rely on providers that apply appropriate safeguards to protect personal data.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account, test and lesson records: for as long as the account is active, and up to 12 months after it is closed, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Unused sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes and are deleted after use.
  • Payment records: up to 6 years, as required for tax and accounting.
  • Teacher applications that are not successful: up to 12 months.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time:

  • Email support@ischool.ng with the subject “Delete my iSchool account”, from the email address on the account (or include the phone number or username used to sign in).
  • See also: How to delete your iSchool account.

We will confirm your request and delete your account, profile, test results and lesson history within 30 days. Payment records we must keep by law are retained only for that purpose.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, access to systems is restricted to authorised staff, and sign-in codes are short-lived and rate-limited. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password and codes private.

9. Children

Our services are designed for secondary school students and are offered through parents, guardians and schools. We collect only the information needed to provide tests, lessons and results; we do not show advertising to students or use their data for marketing. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information without your permission, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Subject to the law, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent where we rely on it, and receive a copy of your data. Contact us at support@ischool.ng. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date and, for significant changes, notify you in the app or by email.
