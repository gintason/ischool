Delete your iSchool account

This page explains how to delete an account created in the iSchool Mobile app or on www.ischool.ng (iSchool Ola and iSchool Ole).

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@ischool.ng with the subject “Delete my iSchool account”.
  2. Send it from the email address on the account, or include the phone number or username you sign in with, so we can find it.
  3. We will reply to confirm, then delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (name, email, phone number, username, password)
  • Your learning profile (class level, subjects)
  • Test answers, scores and result history
  • Live lesson matches, attendance and lesson history
  • For teachers: your application details and uploaded CV

What may be kept

  • Payment records (reference, amount, date) for up to 6 years, as required for tax and accounting.
  • Server security logs, which are removed automatically within 90 days.

You can also ask us to delete only some of your data without closing your account — just say so in your email. See our Privacy Policy for more details.
